AI was used for translation and summarization of the job description in Requirement 1.

*Describe your verification method (ran the test, checked the spec, asked the TA, looked up RFC, cross-checked with the ISTQB syllabus, etc.).*

For test cases, I validated coverage by reviewing functional scenarios and added missing edge cases identified through real-device testing considerations. For the QA/QC mindmap, I compared it with QA/QC mindmap from: [12.1: Principles of Quality Assurance (QA) and Quality Control (QC)](https://bio.libretexts.org/Courses/West_Los_Angeles_College/Biotechnology/12:_Quality_Assurance_and_Quality_Control_in_Biotechnology/12.01:_Principles_of_Quality_Assurance_(QA)_and_Quality_Control_(QC)). For defect reports, I verified all information against reliable sources such as NVD and vendor advisories, and additionally cross-checked with another AI model for consistency. Any unsupported or potentially hallucinated content was explicitly highlighted instead of being accepted as factual.
